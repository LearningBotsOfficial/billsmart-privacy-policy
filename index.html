Made for Indian businesses. Free plan available. Your data is never deleted or locked.

Everything your counter needs

One app for the whole day: selling, stocking, collecting money and checking how the business is doing.

Invoices and orders

Pick a customer, add products and create a professional invoice in a few taps.

  • Search invoices by number or customer
  • Save as PDF or share on WhatsApp
  • Print with a connected printer
  • Your business name and UPI ID on every bill

Products and categories

Add products with buy and sell price, group them by category and search in a tap.

  • Create, rename and delete your own categories
  • Sell by the piece or by the full box or carton
  • Prices and profit stay hidden on the product list

Stock tracking

Turn on stock for any product and see what is left, in boxes and loose pieces.

  • Add stock, remove damaged items or set a recount
  • Low stock alerts at a level you choose
  • Every stock change is recorded with a note

Customers, routes and villages

Keep names, phone numbers and areas together, and group customers by delivery route and village.

  • Search by name, phone or area
  • Filter customers by route
  • See orders, total sales and last order for each customer
  • Call a customer with one tap

Customer ledger

Track credit without a notebook. See what customers owe you and what they paid.

  • Tap You gave or You got, and the balance updates
  • Running balance after every entry
  • Remind on WhatsApp or SMS with the amount and your UPI ID
  • Filter by You will get, You will give or Settled

Pending payments

See all unpaid dues in one list so you know exactly who to follow up with.

Dashboard and reports

Check sales and profit on the dashboard, then open reports for the full picture.

  • Overview, daily, monthly and product views
  • Total revenue, profit, orders and average order

Business settings

Set up your shop once and it appears on your bills.

  • Business name, type and owner name
  • Phone number and optional GSTIN
  • Choose your app language
