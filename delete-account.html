Account Deletion Request

BillSmart allows users to request deletion of their account and associated data.

How to Request Deletion

Email us from your registered Google account and include the subject:

Delete My BillSmart Account

📧 Email:
billsmartapp@gmail.com

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Your BillSmart account information associated with Google Sign-In.
  • Your email address stored for authentication purposes.

What Data Is Not Stored on Our Servers

  • Invoices and billing history remain on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Deleting your account does not automatically remove local files stored on your device.

Deletion Timeframe

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days of verification.
