Delete Account

BillSmart allows users to request deletion of their account and associated data. To request account deletion, please contact us using the email below from your registered Google account.

How to Request Account Deletion

  • Send an email from your registered Google account.
  • Use the subject: Delete My BillSmart Account.
  • Include your registered email address in the message.

📧 Email:
billsmartapp@gmail.com

What Data Will Be Deleted

  • Your BillSmart account associated with Google Sign-In.
  • Your email address and account information stored by BillSmart.
  • Your invoices and billing history stored in BillSmart's cloud services.

Data Retention

  • Invoice and billing history may be retained for a limited period depending on your subscription plan and app settings.
  • Some invoice data may be automatically deleted according to the retention period applicable to your account.

Deletion Timeframe

Account deletion requests are processed within 30 days of verification. Once your account is deleted, your cloud-stored invoices and associated account data will no longer be accessible.
